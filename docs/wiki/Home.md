# Gluon wiki

Gluon runs compatible Windows Steam games on Apple silicon Macs. Start with setup, then use the troubleshooting and graphics guides if a game needs attention.

- [Getting started](Getting-Started.md) covers installing, updating, repairing, and uninstalling Gluon.
- [Troubleshooting](Troubleshooting.md) explains the Steam launch stages and helps diagnose installer, game, window, and Cloud problems.
- [Graphics and compatibility](Graphics-and-Compatibility.md) lists the available backends and per-game settings.
- [Support](../../SUPPORT.md) explains how to share the relevant logs and open a report.

For contributors, see [cloud development](../CLOUD-DEVELOPMENT.md), [Steam Play integration](../STEAMPLAY.md), and the [runtime reference](../RUNTIME.md).
